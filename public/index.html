Printing it
  • Every plate is held to 4 filaments (or 5 if you switch it): when a design or your picks would need more, the least-used colors share the nearest remaining filament, and the swatch says "shared".
  • Each color is a separate part. The Bambu file opens with every part already assigned to filament slots 1, 2, 3…; load your AMS in the order shown under Polymaker colors.
  • The README that comes with each save lists every part's Polymaker filament and color, the grams it needs, and Polymaker's recommended nozzle, bed, speed, fan, retraction and drying settings.
  • Print flat, face up, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers (0.12 mm for small letters). No supports.
  • Saving gives you the .3mf and a README.txt. On a phone it opens the share sheet so you can send both to Files, AirDrop or your printer app. The STL is the whole plate as one part, for single-color printing.
  • Vehicle front ends are original era-styled designs. They don't reproduce any carmaker's badge or a specific model's grille, and the TV themes use genre art rather than show logos or characters.
